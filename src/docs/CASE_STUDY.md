# VitaBloom Health — Case Study

## Overview

**VitaBloom Health** is a web platform for senior living and post-acute operators: a public marketing site plus a staff dashboard for residents, medications, scheduling, billing, family engagement, and analytics.

## Goals

- Replace fragmented tools with one accountable operating layer.
- Keep language and UI calm for bedside teams under pressure.
- Meet expectations for HIPAA-aligned handling of sensitive data.

## Outcomes

VitaBloom Health unifies day-to-day workflows so leaders see variance early and staff spend less time reconciling systems.

## Brand & Assets

- Meta tags, favicon, and social preview graphics use the VitaBloom Health identity.
- Visual system: deep teal and restrained brass accents with soft gradient surfaces — professional, not playful.
